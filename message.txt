In the quiet moments  
between two busy days,  
there’s a strange clarity  
that settles in.  

The noise of plans,  
deadlines,  
and conversations fades,  

and what remains is a softer rhythm —  
the hum of a ceiling fan,  
the faint scent of rain  
on warm concrete,  
the feeling of your own breath  
finding its pace.  

It’s here you realize  
that life is rarely about  
the big, cinematic moments we imagine;  

it’s made of these small,  
almost invisible fragments.  

The sip of tea  
before it cools.  

The shared glance  
with someone who understands  
without words.  

The slow light of dawn  
spilling across your room.  

We spend so much time  
chasing the extraordinary  
that we forget —  

the extraordinary is quietly hidden  
in the ordinary,  
waiting for us to notice.
